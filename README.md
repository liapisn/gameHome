# gameHome

**Walk through a home before you visit it.**

People choose homes from photos, and photos hide the layout: how big the bedroom really is, where the kitchen sits next to the living room, how the rooms connect. 3D tours fix that, but today they need scanning hardware and an operator, so most listings never get one.

gameHome turns what a listing already has, a floor plan and ordinary photos, into a walkable 3D tour. No camera rig, no site visit. Every home that has a floor plan can be explored room by room, at its real size.

## Built with Prosperty

[Prosperty](https://theprosperty.com) is a sponsor of the GenAI Summit hackathon, and its listings power our demo: paste a Prosperty listing link, and gameHome brings in the floor plans and photos, reads the rooms and matches each photo to its room. One press of **Generate** then builds a 3D tour of that home.
